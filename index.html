Inamigo Foundation

InAmigos is an India-based youth-led nonprofit organization that focuses on community development through volunteering, education, hunger relief, women's empowerment, environmental initiatives, and skill development. It describes itself as a Section 8 company (a not-for-profit company under Indian law) and states that it has certifications such as 80G, 12A, CSR-1, ISO 9001:2015, and NGO Darpan registration.


Our foundation is also CSR-1 registered, allowing us to collaborate with corporate partners for impactful Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) initiatives. Additionally, we are NITI Aayog registered, aligning our work with national development goals and hold the prestigious IAF ISO 9001:2015 certification, signifying our commitment to maintaining high-quality standards in operations and service delivery.

What they do



Their major initiatives include:


  1. Education: Teaching children in underserved communities through programs like BalPathshala.


  2. Food & Clothing Distribution: Organizing meal and clothing drives under initiatives such as Project SEVA.


  3. Women Empowerment: Menstrual hygiene awareness, vocational training, and livelihood support.


  4. Environmental Work: Tree plantation and sustainability campaigns.


  5. Skill Development: Training students and volunteers in leadership and professional skills.

Spotlight


InAmigos Foundation is a non-profit organization dedicated to creating positive social impact through initiatives in education, women empowerment, environmental conservation, community welfare, animal welfare, and skill development. Through Project BachpanSala, the foundation provides educational support, digital learning opportunities, and learning materials to children from underprivileged communities, helping them build a brighter future. Project Udaan empowers women by offering vocational training, financial literacy programs, entrepreneurship guidance, and awareness sessions that promote self-reliance and confidence.

The foundation also works towards environmental sustainability through Project Prakriti, which organizes tree plantation drives, environmental awareness campaigns, and promotes eco-friendly practices. Project Jeev focuses on the welfare of stray and abandoned animals by providing food, rescue services, and medical care while encouraging compassion toward animals. Through Project Seva, the organization supports vulnerable communities by distributing food, clothing, and essential supplies during times of need and emergencies.

To enhance employability among youth, Project Vikas offers internship opportunities, career guidance, digital skills training, resume-building workshops, and practical learning experiences in various fields. Together, these projects reflect InAmigos Foundation's mission to build an inclusive, sustainable, and compassionate society by empowering individuals, supporting communities, and encouraging active volunteer participation across India.
